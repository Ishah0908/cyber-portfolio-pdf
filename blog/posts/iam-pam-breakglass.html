IAM/PAM Break-Glass Playbook

Published June 3, 2026 · 6 min read

Emergency access is necessary, but unmanaged break-glass accounts become attacker shortcuts. Treat emergency credentials as controlled explosives: sealed, logged, and time-bound.

1. Break-Glass Design Checklist

Use hardware-backed MFA, dual-approval workflows, and short credential validity windows. Enforce post-incident review within 24 hours.

2. Audit and Verification Commands

# Linux auth trace review
sudo ausearch -m USER_AUTH,USER_ACCT -ts recent

# Quick privilege inventory
getent group sudo

# Cloud IAM policy snapshot (example)
gcloud projects get-iam-policy PROJECT_ID --format=json > iam-snapshot.json

# Diff policy changes
jq '.bindings' iam-snapshot.json

3. Incident Logging Requirements

Capture ticket ID, approving authority, accessed assets, actions taken, and session recording references. Rotate and vault all temporary credentials immediately after closure.
