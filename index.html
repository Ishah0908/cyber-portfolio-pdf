About Ibrahim Sultan

I am Ibrahim Sultan. I hold a Masters in Computer Application and a Masters in Information Systems Security from University of the Cumberlands. I have spent almost a decade in cybersecurity, building practical security programs that combine strong engineering with real-world operations.

I have worked across multiple domains including Oil and Gas, AI, Banking, and Wealth Management. Beyond cybersecurity, I love travelling and building electronics from scratch, including drone projects and an AI-powered robotic mini car designed to navigate rough outdoor trails and wooded paths. I enjoy turning ideas into working prototypes, then hardening them with secure-by-design principles.

I live in the United States and feel blessed to be here in the land of the brave.

My approach to security is rooted in depth and execution: understand the architecture, map the threat path, and build controls that hold up under pressure. I focus on making security measurable and operational, not just conceptual, so teams can move quickly while staying resilient. I believe strong cybersecurity comes from clear design, disciplined implementation, and continuous learning.

This portfolio reflects both sides of my journey: enterprise-scale cybersecurity work and hands-on builder creativity. Whether I am improving identity governance, engineering detection content, hardening cloud environments, or building autonomous mini robotics projects, the mindset is the same: build with intent, test rigorously, and keep improving every cycle.
